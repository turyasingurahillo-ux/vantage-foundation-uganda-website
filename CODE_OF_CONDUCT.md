# Community Code of Conduct

Vantage Foundation Uganda expects contributors, maintainers, volunteers, and collaborators to participate respectfully, professionally, and safely.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas, code, content, or processes rather than people;
- welcome contributors regardless of background, experience, identity, or role;
- protect confidential, personal, beneficiary, donor, applicant, staff, and safeguarding information;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized conduct, or deliberate humiliation;
- respect safeguarding and media-consent requirements when discussing Vantage programmes or communities;
- report security vulnerabilities and sensitive incidents through private channels rather than public issues.

## Unacceptable conduct

Abuse, harassment, discriminatory behavior, doxxing, publication of private information, malicious disclosure of credentials or vulnerabilities, and conduct that places community members or programme participants at risk are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions and may restrict participation where conduct violates this policy or creates a safeguarding, privacy, security, or community risk.

Concerns about contributor conduct should be raised privately through Vantage Foundation Uganda's official contact channel at https://www.vantagefoundationuganda.com/contact.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, documentation, and other project spaces managed by Vantage Foundation Uganda.
